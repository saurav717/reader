# Koios logo studies

Sixteen concepts for the Koios mark, explored in four rounds on the Design
canvas "Koios logo concepts". J, the bookmark with a star cut out of it, is the
mark now on the site (`public/icons/koios.svg`).

- `studies.png` — the canvas's boards drawn as one sheet: each concept on the
  green tile, on paper and in dark mode, beside the wordmark and at 64, 34 and
  16 px.
- `studies-gpt-image.png` and `ai/` — the same sixteen concepts drawn by
  OpenAI's gpt-image-2 from the boards' captions, one tile each, as a second
  reading of every idea.
- `svg/` — each concept's mark as a standalone SVG in `currentColor`, lifted
  from the "on paper" tile of its board.

| | Concept | The idea |
|---|---|---|
| A | Pole star | The star Koios turns the sky around, over an open book |
| B | Axis K | The stem is the sky's axis, the arm reaches for a star |
| C | Star trails | The sky wheeling around its still centre |
| D | Folio K | The old serif tile, turned into a dog-eared page |
| E | Question star | Koios was the Titan of inquiry; the dot of the question is a star |
| F | Constellation K | Five stars joined into a K, the way a constellation is drawn |
| G | Armillary | The old instrument for charting the heavens: a sphere on its tilted axis |
| H | Star i | A wordmark first: the i in koios is dotted with a star |
| I | Lens | The app is a search box first, so the mark is a magnifier that finds a star |
| J | Bookmark | The place you stopped reading, with a star cut out of it |
| K | Orbit k | A serif k in a sky-ring, with one star set into the orbit |
| L | Needle | A compass needle pointing at the pole star |
| M | Fix | A reticle locked onto a star: a position fix, the way a GPS gets one |
| N | Bearing | A compass ring with four cardinal ticks and a needle that points at a star on the rim |
| O | Satellite | A planet, a tilted orbit, and the satellite on it pinging a star |
| P | Waypoint | A dotted route from where you are to the star you are heading for |

Rounds 1 and 2 (A–H) widen; round 3 (I–L) looks at search, the page, orbit and
the needle; round 4 (M–P) is the space-navigation set. Of the sixteen, A, J and
I say what the product does (find, read, keep); M, N, L and P say what the name
means.
