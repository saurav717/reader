3 · Stabilizing training of sparse models

Sparse models often suffer from training instabilities worse than those observed in standard densely-activated Transformers. We find that the router z-loss resolves these instabilities while slightly improving model quality.

Our router z-loss penalizes large logits into the gating network, and we find it improves stability without quality degradation. We also observe that it is more effective than the alternatives we tried: update clipping, removing multiplicative interactions, and injecting noise…

Table 4 shows that the z-loss improves quality and stability at the 269B scale, where the auxiliary load-balancing loss alone was not sufficient to prevent divergence.
