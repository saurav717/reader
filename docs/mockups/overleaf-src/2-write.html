2  Related work

Load balancing. Most MoE models keep experts busy with an auxiliary loss on the router [9, 37]. Its weight trades balance against quality: too small and experts collapse, too large and the LM loss suffers [37]. Expert choice routing [36] side-steps this by letting experts pick tokens. Closest to us, Wang et al. [31] add a per-expert bias that is used only for selection, never in the gate value.
