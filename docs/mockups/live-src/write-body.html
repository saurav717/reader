\section{Related work}
\paragraph{Load balancing.}
Most MoE models keep experts busy with an auxiliary loss on
the router~\cite{fedus2021switch,zoph2022stmoe}.
Its weight trades balance against quality: too small and
experts collapse, too large and the LM loss
suffers~\cite{zoph2022stmoe}.
Expert choice routing side-steps this by letting experts pick
tokens.

\paragraph{Routing at scale.}
GShard~\cite{lepikhin2020gshard} trained a 600B-parameter MoE
with top-2 gating.
Top-1 routing was later shown to be enough, and cheaper, at
the same quality~\cite{fedus2021switch}, and DeepSeek-V3 drops the
auxiliary loss altogether

\paragraph{Bias-based balancing.}
Closest to us, a per-expert bias used only for selection,updated from the load each batch, balances without a loss.

2  Related work

Load balancing. Most MoE models keep experts busy with an auxiliary loss on the router [9, 37]. Its weight trades balance against quality: too small and experts collapse, too large and the LM loss suffers [37]. Expert choice routing side-steps this by letting experts pick tokens.

Routing at scale. GShard [18] trained a 600B-parameter MoE with top-2 gating. Top-1 routing was later shown to be enough, and cheaper, at the same quality [9], and DeepSeek-V3 drops the auxiliary loss altogether

Bias-based balancing. Closest to us, a per-expert bias used only for selection, updated from the load each batch, balances without a loss.
