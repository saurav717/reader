What this is

The app searches public indexes of research papers, opens papers from the places that publish them, keeps what you collect in your Google Drive, shows your Google Calendar beside them, and can run code and compile a paper for you. A small proxy, run by me, fetches what a browser cannot and runs a few features on the site's own keys for the people signed in.
